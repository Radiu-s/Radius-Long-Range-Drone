# Build Note

These are my personal notes from building the Radius Long Range Drone.  
Just the things I noticed, learned, and would pay attention to next time.

---

## 🧱 Frame & Weight

- The HSKRC Mark4 frame is solid but definitely on the heavier side.
- My final AUW ended up around **1400g**, which is normal for a 21700 Li-ion 6S2P setup.
- **IMPORTANT:** Rear CG works WAY better.  
  If the battery sits too forward, the drone flies like it’s constantly nose-diving.

---

## 🔌 Motor Wiring & ESC Notes

- I shortened all motor wires to reduce vibration and keep the build clean.
- ESC pads on the GEPRC stack are a bit fragile — don’t press too hard or you will lift a pad.
- Keeping wires tight and short helped reduce noise in iNav.

---

## 📡 Walksnail Avatar Setup

- Walksnail gets warm but nothing extreme; 9–12V regulated supply works perfectly.
- Mounting the VTX slightly higher improved airflow.
- No video noise or interference once I isolated the power line properly.

---

## 🧭 GPS & Compass

- BN-880 locks satellites fast AS LONG AS you keep it away from high-current wires.
- Compass orientation matters a lot.  
  I messed it up once — iNav went crazy until I recalibrated.
- Best position: far back, upper plate, clean sky view.

---

## 🔋 Battery Mounting

- **Do NOT forget CG.**  
  The drone feels completely different with the pack positioned slightly backward.
- 21700 pack is heavy (800–900g), dual strap is mandatory.
- Anti-slip pad + proper strap tension = zero battery movement.

---

## ✈️ First Test Flights

- **REALLY IMPORTANT.** If you want a real Long-Range flight, dont forget the Goggles-Vtx Must be FCC software.
  If its EC mode, It just shares 25 mW. You cant even pass your neighbor's yard...

- Despite being heavy, the drone did not feel underpowered at all.
- iNav POS Hold was surprisingly stable right out of the box.
- HQProp 7x4x3 props fly smooth but drain more power than 2-blades.
- Had no jello or weird vibrations — motor balance was solid.

---

## 🔧 Things I’d Watch Out for Next Time

- Might try 7x3.5x2 props for better efficiency.
- Battery wires could be slightly lighter — every gram counts.
- Protect the ESC from dust; it gets dirty fast during field testing.
- I’d add a bit more foam under the pack to reduce impact stress.

---

## ✔️ Summary

This build is heavy, strong, and extremely stable.  
Most of the “feel” comes down to **CG placement**, clean wiring, and the P42A pack.  
Overall, the drone feels like a long-range tank with smooth flying characteristics.
