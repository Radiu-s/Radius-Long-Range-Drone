# Parts List – Radius Long Range Drone (7")

This is the complete parts list used in the Radius Long Range Drone build.  
Everything here is tested together and confirmed to work perfectly as a long-range setup.

---

## 🧱 Frame

- **HSKRC Mark4 7" Frame**
  - Plenty of space for wiring
  - It is really optimized for weight and stabilization, perfect fits for long-range stability

---

## ⚡ Motors

- **T-Motor F90 2806.5 – 1300KV**
  - High torque, smooth response
  - Ideal KV for 7" props on 6S
  - Stays cool even under heavy load

---

## 🧠 Flight Stack

### **Flight Controller**
- **GEPRC TAKER F405**
  - Enough UARTs for GPS + Crossfire + Walksnail
  - Stable gyro performance

### **ESC**
- **GEPRC 60A BLHeli_S 4-in-1 ESC**
  - 60A is overkill for long-range, which is perfect

---

## 🎥 Video / FPV System

- **Walksnail Avatar HD Pro Kit V2**
  - Really Good Quality At Digital system
  - Great clarity + **NIGHTVISION!**

- **Walksnail Avatar Goggles L**
  - On-screen sharpness + comfort
  - Needs FCC mode for real long-range

---

## 📡 Radio Link

- **TBS Crossfire Nano RX**
  - THE standard for true long-range

---

## 🧭 GPS & Navigation

- **BN-880 GPS + Compass (M8N)**
  - Fast satellite lock
  - Accurate heading when properly positioned

---

## 🔋 Battery System

- **Custom 6S2P Li-ion Pack – Molicel P42A (21700)**
  - 8400 mAh total
  - Low sag
  - Heavy, but perfect for endurance
  - Pack weight: ~820–900g (normal for 21700)

---

## 🌪️ Propellers

- **HQProp 7x4x3**
  - Smooth, stable, predictable
  - Not the most efficient, but very nice feel

---

## 🔌 Connectors & Wiring

- **XT60 main connector**
- 12 AWG silicone wire
- Nickel strips for Li-ion cells
- Heat-shrink, foam padding, battery straps
- Cable shielding for VTX and GPS lines

---

## ✔️ Optional / Small Stuff

- Anti-slip battery pad  
- GPS mount  
- Dual battery straps  
- Zip ties, foam tape, cable guides  

---

## 🟩 Summary

This parts list forms a **heavy, stable, high-endurance** 7" long-range build  
with excellent video quality, strong radio link, and great GPS reliability.
