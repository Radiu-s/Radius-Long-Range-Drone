# Custom Li-ion Pack – Molicel P42A 6S2P (21700)

This pack is one of the most “hands-on” parts of the entire build.  
I built it myself using 12× Molicel P42A 21700 cells, arranged in a 6S2P configuration.  
Below is everything I learned while putting it together the good, the annoying, and the actually useful stuff.

---

## 🔋 Why P42A?

I’ve used LiPos for years, but for long-range you eventually accept reality:  
**Li-ion just flies longer.**

The Molicel P42A is basically the "balanced" cell in the 21700 world:

- 4200 mAh per cell  
- High discharge capability  (not more than li-po, more than other cells.)
- Low internal resistance  
- Doesn’t sag like crazy  
- Runs cool  
- Actually safe compared to most cells

In short: It’s the best balance between endurance and power for a 7" LR build.

---

## ⚙️ Pack Configuration – 6S2P

The pack is a simple 6S2P:

- **6 cells in series** for 25.2V full charge  
- **2 cells in parallel** for 8400 mAh

12 cells in total.

This config gives you that sweet spot where the drone feels strong enough to maneuver,  
but also cruises forever without dipping voltage instantly.

---

## 🧱 Physical Build & Layout

The layout is the classic “two parallel blocks stacked into a 6S line.”  
Nothing fancy  just clean and compact.

What I used:

- Pure nickel strip (spot welded, not soldered to cells)  
- 12 AWG silicone wire  
- XT60 connector  
- Thick heat-shrink  
- Foam padding under the pack  
- Two battery straps (long-range = no trust issues)

The goal was durability without making it look like a homemade bomb.

---

## ⚡ Electrical Behavior

Here’s what the pack behaves like on a 7" long-range rig:

- Nominal voltage: **22.2V**  
- Full charge: **25.2V**  
- Actual usable capacity: around **7600–7800 mAh**  
- Sag under cruise: very low  
- Handles 20–30A cruise without getting warm

It feels stable.  
---

## ✈️ Realistic Flight Performance

With my AUW around **1400g** and HQProp 7x4x3 props, here’s what I actually expect:

- **28–34 minutes** normal long-range flying  
- 23–28 minutes if you're hammering the throttle  
- Up to ~35 minutes if you're cruising gently

This pack isn’t lightweight (it’s 21700), but it gives endurance that most LiPos can’t.

---

## 🌡️ Temperature & Sag

The P42A cells stay surprisingly cool during cruise.  
Only during full throttle climbs do they warm up a bit, nothing alarming.

Voltage sag is minimal.  
---

## 🛡️ Safety Stuff (What Actually Matters)

Some real-world tips from building and flying Li-ion:

- Never charge unattended  
- Charge as Li-ion, NOT LiPo balance mode  
- Store around 3.6–3.7V per cell  
- Don’t push below 3.2V under load  
- Avoid bending, crushing, or moisture  
- If a cell gets dented → retire it, don’t argue with it

21700 packs are tougher than 18650,  
but still not magic.

---

## 🧭 Mounting on the Drone

Since the pack is heavy (~820–900g), I mount it:

- Longitudinally on the top plate  
- On a grippy anti-slip pad  
- With **two** straps  
- With the GPS placed far enough to avoid interference
– **NEVER forget the CG (Center of Gravity).**
- Always push the pack as far back on the frame as possible; this drone flies like trash if the CG creeps forward.
CG matters a LOT on a 7" rig; this layout keeps pitch stable.

---

## 🧰 Maintenance Tips

- Check IR every ~20 cycles  
- Inspect heat-shrink and padding  
- Keep connectors clean  
- If the pack ever feels “soft” or loses capacity fast, retire it

These packs last a long time if treated right.

---

## ✔️ Summary (Straight to the Point)

The Molicel P42A 6S2P pack gives:

- Long flight time  
- Minimal sag  
- Good temperature behavior  
- Reliable power for long-range  
- Proper efficiency for 7" cruising

It’s heavier than LiPo, sure but for endurance, nothing beats it.

This pack transformed the feel of the drone,  
and it’s the reason it can stay in the air for half an hour.
